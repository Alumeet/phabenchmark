The directory

These seed entries were added by the editor from public sources; the makers have not reviewed them. Submissions are open; maker-submitted entries say so on the card.

The benchmark, when it exists

Nothing here has been benchmarked. Ground rules:

  1. Directory and benchmark stay separate. A listing means only "this exists" - never that an agent has been tested.
  2. Methodology published before results. The reviewing mechanism and criteria are published at How we review, adapted from Google Research's personal health agent evaluation (Nature, 2026).
  3. Testing only with the maker's knowledge. An agent gets a structured review only after its maker has agreed, and every result is labeled with the method, date, and limits of the test.

Community reviews

Anyone can submit a review of a listed agent. Reviews are individual experiences, held privately for moderation, and published only after a person approves them. They are not tests, expert ratings, or benchmark results - those live under How we review and nothing has been formally reviewed yet.
